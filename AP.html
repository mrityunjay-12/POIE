About The Program

To be considered for admission, students are required to submit their academic records, application details, and supporting documents. The admission team reviews every application carefully to ensure the right academic fit.

Program Highlights

  • Simple and transparent application process.
  • Document verification and admission counselling.
  • Guidance from enquiry to final enrolment.
  • Support for academic and career progression.

Course Outline

Students submit basic details and connect with the admission team for initial guidance.

Applicants complete the admission form and submit the required documents.

The admission team verifies academic records, identity proof, and eligibility documents.

Students receive counselling related to programme structure, eligibility, and learning pathway.

After approval, students complete formalities and begin their academic journey.
