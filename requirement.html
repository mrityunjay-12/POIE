About The Program

To be considered for the extended diploma in Business or Information Technology ,applicants must meet at least one of the following academic criteria:

Program Highlights

  • A minnimum of 55% in CBSE Grade X, Cambridge O level, or an equivalent secondary school qualification.
  • Preferred subjects include Science , Mathematics ,Commerce or Technical subjects depending on the chosen programme.
  • Successful completion of a UAE national qualification at Level 5 or above , as recognized by the Ministry of Education .
  • Applicants who do not fully meet these academic criteria but hold relevant professional experience or alternative qualifications will be considered on a case-by-case basis by the admission panel.

Applicants aged 21 or over who do not hold standard entry qualifications may be considered via the mature entry route. Please contact the Admissions team to discuss your individual circumstances.
