Visa Assistance

We are committed to empowering our students, faculty, and staff by fostering positive change in both their personal and professional lives.

Overview

Planet One International Education’s approved Pearson BTEC centre in Ras Al Khaimah will sponsor your student or trainee visa, provided you have confirmed your enrolment on a Pearson BTEC programme and formally requested visa sponsorship through Student Services.

Our team manages the entire submission process on your behalf, in collaboration with the UAE authorities.

  • A clear colour copy of your valid passport — must remain valid for at least six months beyond the date of your visa application
  • A recent passport-size photograph taken against a plain white background, taken within the last three months
  • A certified copy of your Higher Secondary School certificate or equivalent leaving qualification
  • Proof of enrolment / offer letter from the Pearson BTEC approved centre

Academic Document Attestation Requirements

Educational Certificates

Original Grade 10 mark sheet or pass certificate.

Attested by Home Country

By the Ministry of Education in your home country.

Attested by UAE Authorities

By the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the UAE (attestation / apostille).

Submit to Student Services

Documents that have not been attested will be returned unprocessed.
